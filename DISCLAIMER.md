## Reading and comprehension

Flashread is a reading aid. It is not an educational, medical, or therapeutic tool, and it makes no claim to improve reading ability, memory, or learning outcomes.

Research on rapid serial visual presentation (RSVP) is mixed. Reading one word at a time removes the ability to glance back, and comprehension usually drops as speed rises. Don't rely on Flashread for material where missing a detail matters, such as contracts, legal or medical documents, instructions, exams, or code. Read those normally.

## Health and comfort

Flashread shows words in quick succession in one spot. It doesn't strobe or flash colors, but some people may find rapid text tiring or uncomfortable.

- Stop and rest if you notice eye strain, headache, dizziness, or nausea.
- If you have epilepsy, a history of seizures, or a visual or vestibular condition, check with a medical professional before using rapid-presentation tools.
- Don't use it while driving, walking, or doing anything else that needs your attention.

## Privacy and data

- Text you paste, open, or drop is processed only in your browser. Flashread has no server, no account, no analytics, and no tracking.
- Your text, settings, and reading position are saved in your browser's `localStorage` on your device. Anyone with access to that browser profile can see them. Use **Clear** to remove them, or clear your browser's site data.
- The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard web request data (such as IP address and browser type) when the page loads. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- Browser storage isn't backed up. Keep your own copy of anything you care about.
